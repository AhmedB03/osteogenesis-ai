# Verified research bibliography

Links and identifiers checked against publisher/PubMed/PMC records on 2026-11-06. These sources support qualitative biology, **not** any MVP parameter value or adult regeneration efficacy. No experimental dataset has been ingested.

1. Vortkamp A et al. **Regulation of rate of cartilage differentiation by Indian hedgehog and PTH-related protein.** Science (1996), 273:613–622. DOI [10.1126/science.273.5275.613](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.273.5275.613). PMID [8662546](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8662546/). Primary developmental study; basis for qualitative IHH/PTHrP feedback.
2. Mizuhashi K et al. **Resting zone of the growth plate houses a unique class of skeletal stem cells.** Nature (2018). DOI [10.1038/s41586-018-0662-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0662-5). [Open manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6251707/). Primary mouse lineage-tracing study; resting-zone source for columnar chondrocytes.
3. **BMPs regulate multiple aspects of growth-plate chondrogenesis through opposing actions on FGF pathways.** Primary mouse genetic study (2006). PMID [17065231](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17065231/). Qualitative BMP/FGF pathway interactions; no numerical coefficient transferred.
4. Hallett SA et al. **Chondrocytes in the resting zone of the growth plate are maintained in a Wnt-inhibitory environment.** eLife (2021), 10:e64513. DOI [10.7554/eLife.64513](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.64513). PMID [34309509](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34309509/). Primary mouse experiments; illustrates compartment-specific effects omitted from the MVP.
5. **IGF-1R Signaling in Chondrocytes Modulates Growth Plate Development by Interacting With the PTHrP/Ihh Pathway.** Primary mouse conditional-deletion study. [PMC3530140](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3530140/). Role of chondrocyte IGF-1R in development. DOI/PMID not added because not independently verified during this implementation.

6. Schrier L et al. **Depletion of resting zone chondrocytes during growth plate senescence.** Journal of Endocrinology (2006), 189:27–36. DOI [10.1677/joe.1.06489](https://doi.org/10.1677/joe.1.06489). PMID [16614378](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16614378/). Primary rabbit study; supports investigating resting-pool depletion, not a specific exponential decay constant.

## Parameter provenance policy

Every current parameter and saturation constant has origin `illustrative_assumption`. A future calibrated parameter record should include source identifier, exact figure/table, organism, age, tissue zone, assay, units, extraction procedure, uncertainty and applicability limits. A paper mentioning a pathway is insufficient to calibrate its kinetic coefficient. Keep calibration datasets separate from validation datasets.
